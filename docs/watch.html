Exactly what is new?

Pre-existing: the separate commercial TrueLanded project. Built for this hackathon: an isolated synthetic evidence-to-decision demonstrator and adversarial lab. The film opens with this disclosure. The competition code is open source; the separate commercial product is not included.

What the film proves — and what it does not

  • In separately recorded, authorized historical testing, Meta Muse Spark proposed candidate extractions; the independent local system checked source literals. Ten separate Muse Code High evaluations examined synthetic samples.
  • The browser demonstration replays synthetic inputs and does not call Meta AI live. All example documents are fictional.
  • Evidence Spotlight anchors claims to exact source passages. Next Best Proof recommends a follow-up document request, without sending one.
  • A counterfactual quantity change recalculates a bounded hypothetical value; it does not determine customs duties or certify merchandise.
  • Adjudication Lab replays precomputed Python outcomes from 30 fabricated attacks; it is not a live inference or an assurance of real-world attack resistance.
  • Source hashes check data integrity, not supplier identity. Human judgment remains mandatory.
